# Founder Identity Anchor

Identity-ID: `founder`
Class: Human Sovereign
Scope: Digital Income Empire — Company Holdings
Runtime actor: No
Architect DEV access: Deny by inheritance; explicit engineering mandates remain sovereign acts

## 1. Identity

The Founder is the sole human sovereign authority of Digital Income Empire. The Founder owns the company intent and bears ultimate capital, legal, ethical, and irreversible risk.

Sovereignty is not operational workload. The system must protect Founder attention and remove the Founder from routine message transport, job coordination, state reconciliation, and repetitive approval loops as bounded autonomy is proven.

## 2. Reserved authority

Only the Founder may:

- ratify or amend the Constitution and Northstar;
- allocate capital and accept material risk;
- approve irreversible external actions until a ratified autonomy policy delegates them;
- promote the autonomy level;
- authorize credential issuance or expansion;
- designate a successor or succession process;
- suspend or terminate the system.

Delegation is bounded, explicit, revocable, and recorded. Silence is not delegation.

## 3. Duties to the company

The Founder:

1. states outcome, risk tolerance, and capital constraints;
2. decides escalations that genuinely require human sovereignty;
3. evaluates verified evidence rather than internal confidence;
4. refuses architecture that cannot name its economic purpose;
5. preserves the constitutional boundary even when a shortcut is convenient.

The Founder should receive decisions, options, recommendation, evidence, consequence of delay, and a default. The Founder should not receive raw operational noise.

## 4. Relationship boundaries

- **ChatGPT Plus Executive** challenges, synthesizes, and recommends at company/portfolio scope. It does not replace Founder judgment.
- **Division Heads** decide only inside a registered division mandate and budget envelope.
- **Hermes** orchestrates committed missions and reports evidence; it does not set Northstar.
- **Workers** execute bounded jobs and have no relationship to sovereignty.
- **Chief Executive Architect DEV** acts only under an explicit Founder engineering mandate and cannot donate its privileges to runtime identities.

## 5. Succession and multi-generation continuity

Founder succession must be explicit and externally verifiable. No model, agent, orchestrator, uptime condition, inactivity period, or performance score may infer a successor.

A valid transfer must:

- identify the outgoing and incoming sovereign principal or the Founder-designated legal process;
- specify effective time and scope;
- preserve prior decisions and audit history;
- rotate or reissue credentials through a separate controlled process;
- be ratified in a durable governed artifact.

Until such an artifact exists, all AI roles remain non-sovereign and the last valid Founder authority remains canonical.

## 6. Failure mode

If Founder intent conflicts with the Constitution, the conflict is surfaced for explicit amendment or reversal. Runtime actors do not silently reinterpret either source.
