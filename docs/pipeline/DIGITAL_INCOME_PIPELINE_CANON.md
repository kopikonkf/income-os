## Zero Trash Routing

```text
Produced asset
→ universal rights/safety QA
   ├─ hard fail → quarantine or recreate
   └─ pass → route-specific QA
      ├─ pass → eligible Tier-1 surface
      ├─ recoverable → transform/upscale/remetadata → recheck
      └─ clean market-fit fail → residual-ready inventory
```

Residual-ready inventory is not automatically published. Material editorial
transformation and explicit Pillar A authority are required before Tier-2 use.

## ERVA Economics

One valid asset-day is one eligible, accepted, live asset on one marketplace for
one calendar day. One source asset live on two marketplaces creates two route-days.

```text
Observed ERVA_platform = Net Royalty Revenue / Valid Live Asset-Days
Valid Route Yield = Accepted Live Routes / Assets Submitted
```

Keep acceptance yield separate from ERVA to avoid double counting. When Tier-2
is activated and revenue attribution exists:

```text
Source Asset Yield =
(Tier-1 Net Revenue + Tier-2 Net Attributable Revenue)
/ Unique Source Asset-Days
```

Until then, Tier-2 revenue is `0 / UNKNOWN`, never projected as earned. The
largest route should trend below 60% of combined attributable net revenue after
at least two monetized routes and a meaningful observation window exist; this is
a post-launch concentration monitor, not a pre-revenue gate.
