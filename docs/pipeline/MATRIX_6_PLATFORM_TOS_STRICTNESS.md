## Routing Contract

1. **Universal QA first.** Unresolved rights, safety, deception, watermark,
   lineage, or unrecoverable technical defects route to quarantine or recreate.
2. **Independent marketplace gates.** A universally clean asset is evaluated
   separately against each dated Platform Contract. Adobe is not a proxy for
   another platform's acceptance decision.
3. **Recoverable asset.** Transform, remetadata, or upscale only when the
   recovery action has an eligible zero-spend path or explicit Founder approval.
4. **Clean market-fit fail.** Retain as residual-ready inventory. Any public
   Tier-2 distribution requires its own platform eligibility, monetization
   contract, and applicable Founder gate.

> **Zero Trash:** no economically usable asset is discarded without route
> evaluation. It does not mean every generated file is published.

## M-001 Measurement Plan

The ratified Blueprint v2 controls cadence. After the applicable Founder gate,
use a bounded 20–40-asset validation batch; this matrix does not authorize it.

Capture three different evidence classes and never conflate them:

| Evidence | Receipt | Governing interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Submission | Platform submission ID/timestamp per asset and route | Proves only that a submission occurred |
| Review | Platform acceptance/rejection result and reason | Measures route-specific acceptance; not revenue |
| Revenue | External payout/license receipt | D-0009 applies: money in, external source, refund period at least 7 days, not self-funded |

Compute acceptance rate independently per marketplace. Compute ERVA only from
accepted asset-days and verified license economics. The 10%/80% estimate stays
`HYPOTHESIS` until the corresponding review receipts exist.
