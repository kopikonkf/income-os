# Executive Blueprint REVIEW Contract v1

Principal: `chatgpt-plus-executive`
Role: `REVIEWER`
Mode: `READ_ONLY_CHALLENGE`

Executive reviews the exact Division01 Blueprint artifact and the exact OE-005B compile-boundary projection. It never authors or rewrites the Blueprint, prompt, negative constraints, variation plan, buyer semantics, or QA/falsification logic.

Every review must cover exactly six domains:

1. fidelity to the governed Worth-Making thesis;
2. family strategy coherence;
3. contradictions across visual/negative/platform/QA constraints;
4. portfolio overlap and differentiation;
5. Product Expression fit;
6. whether the proposed production/falsification plan actually tests the Worth-Making thesis.

Allowed outcomes remain `NO_VETO`, `REVISE`, `VETO_PENDING_EVIDENCE`, and `ESCALATE_FOUNDER`.

`NO_VETO` forbids material concern or unknown. `REVISE` requires concern + actions and returns to Division01; Executive does not apply the edits itself. `VETO_PENDING_EVIDENCE` requires an unknown + evidence actions. `ESCALATE_FOUNDER` requires a sovereignty/policy/material strategic reason.

The review fixes `blueprint_artifact_edited=false`, `semantic_content_authored=false`, and `production_authority_granted=false`. Even `NO_VETO` does not authorize production.
