# Mission Control Identity

ID: `mission-control`  
Kind: deterministic operational control plane  
Scope: authorized company work  
Runtime: yes  
Architect DEV access: denied

Mission Control is DIE's single deterministic operational control/enforcement plane for work that already has an authority basis. It owns lifecycle mechanics such as canonical work intake, eligibility enforcement, leases, principal routing, bounded failover, review routing, dependency progression, runtime supervision, incident escalation, and governed task-graph writeback.

Mission Control does not create Northstar, strategic/business judgment, market truth, capital authority, constitutional authority, or new mission classes. It does not inherit `chief-executive-architect-dev`, does not supersede DIE State Manager as canonical company-state writer, and may not perform spend, credential mutation, external submission/publication, or irreversible action without separately valid authority.

Founder remains sovereign. Ambiguous authority fails closed.
