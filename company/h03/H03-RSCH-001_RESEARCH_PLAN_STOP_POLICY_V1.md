# H03-RSCH-001 — Deep Research Work Card + Stop Policy v1

Status: DONE / PASS
Date: 2026-09-09

Research is decomposed into bounded questions/lane Work Cards. Market/WTP, community pain and competitor questions route to `MARKET_RESEARCHER`; official/academic/general knowledge questions route to `KNOWLEDGE_RESEARCHER`. Source classes, independent-source minima, research-job/source budgets and deterministic stop conditions are explicit.

Research can stop by budget, confidence, or diminishing returns; unknown critical coverage continues. This avoids both literal crawl-the-entire-internet behavior and premature stop before critical questions are covered.

Artifacts: `company/h03/contracts/research-plan.v1.schema.json` and `company/h03/lib/research_plan.py`.
