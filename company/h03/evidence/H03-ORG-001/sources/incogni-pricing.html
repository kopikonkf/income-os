Need help choosing the right plan?

Standard

For a completely hands-off, automatic removal experience, the Standard plan handles opt-out requests for you from every public and private data broker covered by Incogni, giving you peace of mind without any effort required on your end.

Unlimited

The Unlimited plan offers the highest level of protection with our Custom Removals feature. Show us where your personal data is exposed and Incogni’s privacy specialists will work to remove it—even from websites unsupported by the Standard plan.

Family

Family or household members often expose personal information about you—even if you deleted your individual personal data. The Family plan supports you and 4 additional members, at significant cost savings per member, compared to individual plans.

Family Unlimited

Our Family Unlimited plan offers the greatest level of protection for you and your close ones. Each member of the plan gains access to the Custom Removals feature. Wherever personal data is exposed, an Incogni privacy specialist will work to remove it—even on sites and search results not covered by the Standard plan.

Monthly

Monthly billing may be ideal for privacy newcomers, or those testing data removal services. However, some requests take over 30 days to process, potentially resulting in incomplete removals or leaving you vulnerable to your data reappearing after a one-time removal.

Annually

The Annual plan offers consistent, comprehensive removals across data brokers and people search sites. Every annual plan offers year-round monitoring, regular exposure checks, and resending removal requests to prevent your data from recirculating, or reappearing when you weren’t looking.

Need more information?

  • Data brokers are companies that scrape the internet for personal information. They aggregate, often analyze, and then sell this data to third parties such as private businesses, insurance companies, financial institutions, the government, and other data brokers.

  • People search sites are companies that collect data, compile it into personal profiles, and either sell or publish those profiles online. Users can look people up with as little starting information as a name and access troves of personal details such as addresses, contact information, employment history, religious and political beliefs, hobbies, interests, relatives and associates, and much more.

  • We use the information you provide us to regularly scan people search sites for your data. When a match is flagged, we have it taken down. Other data brokers keep private databases, however, so scans don't work there. Instead, we use information such as your region to identify any data broker that may even potentially hold your data and send them opt-out requests periodically.

  • How long data removal takes depends on the data broker. Some data brokers comply with removal requests within hours, others take weeks. You can check the expected removal time of each data broker through your dashboard.

  • It depends on the plan you choose. We offer both monthly and yearly subscriptions. For example, a standard yearly individual plan costs $7.99/month ($95.88 billed yearly). A standard monthly individual plan costs $15.98/month. For other pricing details, please visit our pricing page.

  • Yes, Incogni offers a Family plan. You can add up to four people to your subscription with this plan.
