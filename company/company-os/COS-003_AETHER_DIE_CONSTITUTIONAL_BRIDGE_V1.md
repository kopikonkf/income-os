# COS-003 — Aether ↔ DIE Constitutional Bridge Contract v1

Status: ACCEPTED BRIDGE CONTRACT / NO AETHER ABSORPTION
Date: 2026-09-09
Authority: subordinate to `CONSTITUTION.md` for DIE and to Aether's own Founder-governed DNA inside the Aether estate.

## 1. Purpose

Define a constitutional boundary between Aether and Digital Income Empire (DIE) without merging identities, repositories, memory stores, runtime authority or canonical truth.

This contract does not make Aether the DIE orchestrator, does not make Mission Control the Aether mind, does not transfer Aether DNA into DIE, and does not authorize any Aether runtime/service/memory mutation.

## 2. Dual-domain authority rule

Aether and DIE are two governed domains with the same ultimate human sovereign but different canonical authority stacks.

### Aether domain

```text
Dee explicit approval
  -> north_star.yaml
  -> Genome.md
  -> aether.core.json
  -> Aether governance/approval services
  -> replaceable runtimes/providers/senses/tools
```

Aether's `north_star.yaml` is the sole highest directional authority **inside Aether**. Its Genome and core self-model are Founder-governed and cannot be overridden by an LLM/runtime.

### DIE domain

```text
Founder Dee
  -> CONSTITUTION.md
  -> Founder-ratified decisions / identity canon
  -> DIE State Manager for company operational truth
  -> Mission Control candidate deterministic enforcement plane
  -> bounded principals/workers/executors
```

`CONSTITUTION.md` remains the highest authority **inside DIE**. Aether's North Star, Genome, persona, memory or beliefs do not silently amend DIE governance.

### Cross-domain conflict

If Aether direction and DIE authority conflict:

```text
STOP automatic mutation
-> preserve evidence
-> identify the conflicting authority domains
-> escalate to Founder
-> require explicit Founder-reviewed amendment/mandate
```

Silence, model confidence, historical behavior, or runtime access never resolves the conflict.

## 3. Role separation: interpret/propose → authorize → enforce → execute → learn

### Stage 1 — INTERPRET / PROPOSE

Primary candidate: **Aether strategic cognition**, supported by replaceable AI principals.

Responsibilities:

- interpret long-horizon Founder direction;
- synthesize market/business/economic evidence;
- challenge assumptions and expose uncertainty;
- generate bounded hypotheses, opportunity proposals and improvement proposals;
- ask what evidence would falsify a belief;
- prioritize by evidence, capital efficiency, reversibility, risk-adjusted expected value, feasibility and compounding value.

Aether may autonomously **propose** improvements because its AX2 explicitly allows bounded self-improvement proposals. Proposal is not authority.

Aether must not at this stage:

- spend money;
- create binding external commitments;
- mutate credentials/permissions;
- amend DIE Constitution;
- amend its own North Star/Genome/core identity;
- directly promote beliefs/knowledge/canonical memory without its governance path;
- create Mission Control Work Cards that bypass DIE eligibility/authority gates.

### Stage 2 — AUTHORIZE

Authority comes from the applicable governed domain, not from Aether confidence.

For DIE company work:

```text
Founder / Founder-ratified Constitution
+ authorized canonical source/decision
+ future bounded Economic Governor envelope where separately ratified
= authority basis
```

For Aether identity/mission/value/self-modification/canonical-memory changes:

```text
Aether governance constraints
+ Founder approval where required
= authority basis
```

Current DIE autonomy remains A0. No autonomous spend, new-vendor commitment, credential mutation, market submission/publication, constitutional amendment or irreversible action is granted by COS-003.

### Stage 3 — ENFORCE

Candidate: **Mission Control / Supervisor** after the separate COS-002 promotion gates are satisfied.

Responsibilities:

- validate work eligibility and dependencies;
- enforce authority/red-zone gates;
- acquire/release leases;
- route an accepted owner/reviewer/executor;
- bound retries and failover;
- enforce independent review where required;
- supervise runtime health and incident escalation;
- progress governed work deterministically;
- prevent duplicate/late/stale side effects.

Mission Control does **not** interpret Aether identity, invent Northstar, promote Aether beliefs, allocate capital by itself, or become the canonical writer of Aether or DIE company truth.

Until COS-002/COS-004 promotion is ratified, current DIE canon remains authoritative regarding operational-control-plane identity.

### Stage 4 — EXECUTE

Execution is replaceable and task-scoped.

Candidate execution surfaces include:

- global web/API cognition principals;
- bounded workers such as OpenCode where retained;
- provider/production engines;
- holding-local Architect MCP executors via delegated execution;
- Hermes only where later evidence-based disposition retains a specialist/edge role.

Execution must not inherit authority merely because a tool is technically reachable.

`chief-executive-architect-dev` remains Founder-invoked/non-runtime. Runtime `chatgpt-architect` cognition does not inherit unrestricted DEV authority; proactive privileged execution requires the accepted delegated-execution contract.

### Stage 5 — LEARN

Learning closes the loop but does not bypass governance.

```text
execution / market / incident result
 -> evidence with provenance
 -> DIE State Manager / Economic Ledger / governed evidence store as applicable
 -> sanitized evidence projection
 -> Aether interpretation
 -> belief/knowledge/improvement candidate
 -> Aether governance promotion lifecycle
 -> future proposal
```

Aether's Genome rules apply within Aether:

- Evidence > Confidence;
- Traceable > Accumulated;
- Falsifiable > Comfortable;
- Honest > Impressive;
- beliefs follow a lifecycle rather than being silently accumulated;
- direct belief writes are forbidden;
- canonical memory/knowledge promotion is governed.

Mission Control completion, task count, AI confidence, or revenue estimate is not automatically an Aether validated belief.
