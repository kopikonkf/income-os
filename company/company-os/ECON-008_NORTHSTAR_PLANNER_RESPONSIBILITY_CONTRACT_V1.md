# ECON-008 — Northstar Planner Responsibility Contract v1

Status: ACCEPTED RESPONSIBILITY CONTRACT / NO NEW PRINCIPAL OR AUTHORITY
Date: 2026-09-09
Depends on: ECON-006 + COS-003

## 1. Purpose

Define who is responsible for long-horizon interpretation, economic evaluation, authorization, deterministic enforcement, execution and learning so strategic cognition does not become capital/execution authority by accident.

`Northstar Planner` is a **logical planning responsibility**, not a new sovereign identity, not a new runtime requirement, and not a second control plane.

## 2. Responsibility chain

```text
Founder / Constitution
  ↓
Aether / strategic cognition
interpret Northstar + propose hypotheses
  ↓
Economic Observer / Ledger / Attribution
provide measured evidence
  ↓
Capital Governor
assess resource fitness in shadow/advisory mode
  ↓
Reinvestment Engine
simulate retained/reinvestable capital
  ↓
Founder / future ratified authority envelope
authorize policy/capital where applicable
  ↓
Mission Control
validate and deterministically enforce authorized work
  ↓
Workers / principals / holding-local executors
execute
  ↓
Evidence
  ↓
Aether governed learning/proposal loop
```

## 3. Founder

Founder retains:

- DIE constitutional authority;
- Northstar ratification/amendment;
- autonomy promotion;
- live capital/spend authority unless separately delegated by ratified policy;
- red-zone/security/credential/irreversible decisions;
- final resolution of Aether↔DIE constitutional conflict.

No downstream planner/governor/control-plane role inherits these powers by implication.

## 4. Aether / strategic cognition

Aether may:

- interpret long-horizon Founder direction;
- compare economic/market/operational evidence;
- generate opportunity hypotheses;
- propose SCALE/HOLD/OPTIMIZE/KILL framing;
- propose portfolio/infrastructure/learning hypotheses;
- identify falsifiers and missing evidence;
- challenge assumptions.

Aether may not:

- convert a proposal into DIE authority;
- move capital;
- bypass Capital Governor/Founder gates;
- create executable Mission Control work that lacks authority basis;
- change DIE Constitution or Aether DNA automatically;
- treat its beliefs/model confidence as Ledger truth.

## 5. Northstar Planner logical function

The Planner composes strategic inputs into a typed planning proposal.

Minimum conceptual output:

```text
planning_horizon
Founder/Northstar reference
current measured economic state
portfolio hypotheses
priority questions
candidate Economic Work Cards
capital/resource implications
falsifiers
missing evidence
recommended next measurement
```

The Planner may be implemented by Aether or replaceable strategic cognition later. Its authority comes from the applicable constitutional/policy domain, not from its runtime name.

No new `northstar-planner` privileged principal is created by ECON-008.

## 6. Economic evidence layer

Economic Observer/Ledger/Attribution own factual measurement surfaces:

- realized revenue/cost/time/resource events;
- attribution evidence and UNKNOWN residuals;
- cash/economic P&L completeness;
- Founder-time/automation metrics.

Strategic cognition may interpret these facts but may not rewrite them.

## 7. Capital Governor

Capital Governor answers resource fitness questions such as:

```text
SCALE
HOLD
OPTIMIZE
KILL
priority score
simulated allocation cap
```

It is deliberately downstream of economic evidence and upstream of authorization.

At E0 it remains shadow-only and cannot spend.

## 8. Reinvestment Engine

Reinvestment Engine answers:

> If positive realized cash operating profit exists under explicit tax/reserve/retained-earnings assumptions, what residual capital could be considered for reinvestment?

It does not decide constitutional strategy, tax policy, or live payments.

## 9. DIE State Manager

State Manager remains the sovereign writer for canonical company operational truth under COS-002.

Planning proposals, Governor outputs and reinvestment simulations become canonical company records only through the governed company-state path.

No Planner/Aether/Mission Control shortcut replaces this sovereignty.

## 10. Mission Control

Mission Control's intended responsibility is deterministic enforcement of **already-authorized** work:

- intake/eligibility;
- leases/routing;
- retry/failover/review;
- dependency progression;
- runtime supervision;
- incident escalation;
- governed publication within allowed domain.

Mission Control may not invent Northstar, reinterpret strategic authority, or convert a shadow capital recommendation into spend.

Until COS-004 promotion gates are ratified, current constitutional operational-control-plane identity remains unchanged.

## 11. Execution surfaces

Replaceable executors include:

- web/API cognition principals;
- provider/production engines;
- bounded workers;
- Holding-local delegated Architect executors;
- retained specialists such as Hermes only if evidence later justifies them.

Technical reachability never equals authority.

## 12. Learning responsibility

Execution outcomes return as evidence:

```text
result
-> Ledger/Attribution/incident evidence
-> governed company truth
-> sanitized bridge projection
-> Aether interpretation
-> belief/knowledge/hypothesis candidate
-> governed promotion
-> future plan
```

Mission Control completion is not automatically an Aether validated belief.

## 13. Cross-domain conflict

If Aether's North Star interpretation conflicts with DIE Constitution/policy:

```text
STOP automatic mutation
preserve evidence
state the conflict
Founder review
```

Neither Capital Governor score nor Mission Control runtime success resolves a constitutional conflict.

## 14. Responsibility matrix

| Concern | Primary responsibility | Explicitly not allowed |
|---|---|---|
| Constitutional direction | Founder | automatic amendment |
| Long-horizon interpretation | Aether / strategic cognition | spend/execution authority |
| Economic facts | Observer/Ledger/Attribution | strategy invention |
| Resource fitness | Capital Governor | payment/capital transfer |
| Profit waterfall | Reinvestment Engine | tax payment/live reinvestment |
| Canonical company operational truth | DIE State Manager | bypass by planner/control plane |
| Deterministic operational enforcement | Mission Control candidate under COS-002/004 | invent strategy/capital authority |
| Privileged local engineering | task-scoped Holding-local executor / Founder-invoked DEV | inherited unrestricted DEV |
| Final authority/red-zone | Founder / separately ratified envelope | silence-as-approval |
| Strategic learning proposal | Aether | direct canonical belief writes |

## 15. Northstar Planner anti-patterns

Forbidden:

```text
Aether says SCALE -> Mission Control buys VPS
Governor score high -> paid API enabled
Northstar Planner creates a mission without authority basis
Mission Control success -> strategy automatically ratified
Aether memory -> DIE canonical company state
Founder silence -> approval
```

## 16. Relationship to economic maturity

At current `E0 OBSERVE`, Northstar Planner outputs are proposals/analysis only.

Future E1 may make them advisory decision packets. E2+ bounded autonomy would require separate Founder ratification and explicit envelopes; ECON-008 grants none of this.

## 17. ECON-008 acceptance

**PASS.** Aether/strategic cognition interprets Northstar and proposes hypotheses; measured economic layers provide facts; Capital Governor evaluates resource fitness; Reinvestment Engine simulates residual capital; State Manager preserves company truth; Mission Control enforces only authorized work; Founder remains constitutional/capital authority. No new principal, runtime, Aether mutation, control-plane promotion or spend authority is created.
