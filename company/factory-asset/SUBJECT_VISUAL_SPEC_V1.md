# Subject + Visual Requirement Specification v1

Tasks: `FA-333`, `FA-334`

A provider prompt is not semantic truth. Subject facts and visual art direction are durable typed inputs. The Subject Specification records what must be recognizable and complete; the Visual Requirement Spec resolves that subject against one versioned Production Preset and explicit bounded overrides.

The compiler must not invent missing subject anatomy/components. If a complex subject needs components, cognition or Founder-specified evidence must author them before compilation. This allows post-generation visual QA to compare the artifact against the same requirements that generated it.

For isolated objects, the minimum resolved contract includes subject form/recognition anchors, rendering/style/medium, view, fidelity, full-subject visibility, clean silhouette, background mode, reusable-stock intent and negative constraints.
